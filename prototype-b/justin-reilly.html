Justin M. Reilly, Esq.

Justin M. Reilly represents clients in employment disputes and personal injury litigation, including wage-and-hour claims, discrimination, wrongful termination and serious workplace injuries. His practice includes appellate advocacy.

Mr. Reilly graduated from Quinnipiac University School of Law and served as an Associate Editor of the Quinnipiac Law Review.

Admissions

His published biography lists admission in New York and Connecticut, and to the U.S. District Courts for the Eastern and Southern Districts of New York.

Litigation Experience

His experience includes federal and state employment matters, serious injuries and appeals in the New York appellate courts.
