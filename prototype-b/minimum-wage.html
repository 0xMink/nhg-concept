How Minimum Wage Rules Apply

New York sets minimum wage requirements that can differ by geographic region and industry. Rates change over time; a historical claim must be evaluated using the rate applicable when the work was performed.

Some workers have special rules concerning tips, allowances or other pay practices.

Common Minimum Wage Problems

Unpaid training time, off-the-clock duties, unlawful deductions, inaccurate hours and improper tip practices may affect whether a worker received the legally required pay.

What Records Matter?

Pay statements, time records, schedules, tip records, messages and the dates and locations of employment can be important.

Recovery and Deadlines

Qualifying claims may involve unpaid wages, interest, statutory damages and attorneys’ fees. Different laws may apply, and the correct claim period should be evaluated.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the minimum wage the same everywhere in New York?

No. The applicable rate can depend on region, industry, date and other rules.

Can deductions cause a minimum wage violation?

Yes, some unlawful deductions or pay practices can affect compliance.
