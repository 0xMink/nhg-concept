Before Signing an Agreement

An employment agreement may address compensation, bonuses, commissions, confidentiality, intellectual property, arbitration and restrictions on future work. Employees should understand the obligations before signing.

Severance and Release Agreements

Severance agreements may require a worker to release legal claims or accept confidentiality and other conditions. Whether a particular term is enforceable depends on the facts and law.

Non-Compete and Non-Solicitation Provisions

New York’s treatment of restrictive covenants is fact-specific and can change through legislation and judicial decisions. A clause should not be assumed valid merely because it appears in a signed agreement.

Negotiation and Litigation

NHG’s existing website describes work involving employment agreement negotiation and disputes. The appropriate strategy may involve negotiation, mediation, arbitration or litigation.
