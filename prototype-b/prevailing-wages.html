What Is a Prevailing Wage?

Prevailing wage requirements can apply to covered public work and certain other projects or services. Rates may vary by trade, classification, geographic area and project.

Common Disputes

Workers may be paid the wrong classification rate, receive inadequate supplemental benefits, or be asked to work unrecorded hours. Contract coverage and the applicable wage schedule are central.

What Records Matter?

Project location, public contract information, wage schedules, pay statements, time records and job duties can help evaluate a potential claim.

Potential Remedies

Applicable Labor Law provisions and contractual or other theories may permit recovery. Public work claims can have special procedures, parties and deadlines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does every construction job pay prevailing wages?

No. Coverage depends on the nature of the project and governing law.

How do I know the correct rate?

The applicable public wage schedule, work classification, location and dates must be reviewed.
