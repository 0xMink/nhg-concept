Unpaid Wages or Harassment?

These are two of the most important issues we want visitors to find quickly. Wage claims often involve time records, job duties, pay statements, and Labor Law requirements. Harassment claims require a different analysis under New York State and, where applicable, New York City law.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I ask about unpaid overtime or wages?

Yes. The firm handles wage-and-hour matters, including unpaid overtime and other wage violations. The rules depend on the facts and applicable law.

What if I am concerned about retaliation?

The employment retaliation guide explains how legal protections may apply. Preserve relevant records lawfully and seek advice about your circumstances.

What should I have ready for an initial conversation?

A general description of the issue and approximate dates can help. You do not need to send sensitive employment records through this website.
