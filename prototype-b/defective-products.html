Types of Product Defects

Product liability claims can involve manufacturing defects, defective designs, and inadequate warnings or instructions. The proof differs depending on the alleged defect.

Who May Be Responsible?

Manufacturers and other parties in the distribution chain may face liability under applicable legal theories. A product’s age, modifications, intended uses and available warnings can affect the analysis.

Preserving the Product

The product itself can be critical evidence. If safe and feasible, preserve the item, packaging, manuals, photographs and purchase information. Avoid altering or disposing of it before obtaining legal advice.

Timing and Compensation

New York limitation rules and defenses vary by claim. Medical costs, lost earnings and other damages may be recoverable where the elements of a claim are proven.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a product recall to bring a claim?

No. A recall is not necessarily required, though recall information can be relevant.

Should I throw away the broken product?

Preserving the item is often important. Speak with counsel before altering or disposing of it.
