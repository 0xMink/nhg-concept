Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to share every detail at first?

No. You can begin with a general description and ask how to arrange a conversation with an attorney.

Are there deadlines for abuse-related claims?

Different claims and circumstances have different deadlines. Review the linked legal guides and speak with an attorney about your specific situation.

Can I contact the firm about abuse in a school or workplace?

Yes. NHG has practice guides covering school abuse and workplace abuse. The firm can discuss whether a matter warrants further investigation.

Discuss Your Case Privately.

You do not have to describe every detail in your first conversation. Speak with our team about your questions and the legal options that may be available.
