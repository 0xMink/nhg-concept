Keith E. Williams, Esq.

Keith E. Williams handles plaintiff-side employment litigation and other civil disputes, including wage-and-hour, discrimination and wrongful-termination matters. He also has experience with personal injury, contracts and other tort claims.

Biography adapted from the firm’s published profile; verify current role, bar admissions and practice details before launch.

Background

Mr. Williams served in the United States Air Force. Before joining NHG, his professional background included a judicial internship at the Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals and an externship with the Federal Trade Commission.

Admissions

His published biography lists admissions in New York, New Jersey and California, as well as multiple federal courts.

Practice

Since joining the firm in 2016, he has handled employment claims involving wage disputes, discrimination, FMLA, ADA and other workplace protections.
